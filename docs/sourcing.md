# Polish sourcing: listings checked on 2026-11-11

Raw results of the shop checks behind the parts list. Prices are gross PLN as shown on the day.

**How to read the "checked" column**

- **page** — the shop's own product page was opened and the price and stock read from it.
- **listing** — the shop's own search/category listing was read, not the product page.
- **snippet** — seen only through a search snippet or a price aggregator (elecena.pl, Ceneo). Treat price and stock as unconfirmed.
- **maker** — manufacturer's page with a recommended price; no shop found.

Kamami and Allegro refused automated access (HTTP 403), so nothing from them is better than "snippet". TME opened only partly. Batna24, Wispro, ElectroPark and Nettigo could not be searched.
